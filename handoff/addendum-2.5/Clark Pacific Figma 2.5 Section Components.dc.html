Clark Pacific · Figma 2.5 · Additions only · 30 Sep 2026
Section components 2.5 additions

45 section components that aren't in your Figma file yet, one 1440 frame each. Nothing here replaces or changes what you already have. These are additions only, and every name starts with 2.5 / so they stay grouped and never clash with your existing components.

1 · Save a version

In your Clark Pacific file: File → Save to version history. Name it "Before 2.5".

2 · Add a page

In your file, add a new page called "Components · 2.5 Additions". Don't paste onto your existing Components page.

3 · Copy → paste

Here, click a frame's name in Layers, press ⌘C, then paste it onto the new page with ⌘V.

4 · Create component

Right-click the pasted frame → Create component (⌥⌘K). It turns purple and appears in Assets under "2.5".

Interactive11 components have behavior you can't see in the static frame. The yellow card beside each frame explains it. Paste the card next to the component, but don't make the card itself a component.

Step-by-step instructions with pictures are in "Figma 2.5: How to add the new components". Behavior notes for every module, including the ones already in your file like the FAQ accordion, are in "Figma 2.5 Dev Notes" for your developers.

UC Davis Health needed 1,200 new stalls adjacent to the medical center without disrupting an active campus. Clark Pacific delivered the structure design-build — precast frame, architectural facade panels, and a photovoltaic-ready roof deck — fourteen weeks after mobilization.

Off-site fabrication ran parallel to foundations, cutting roughly five months from a conventional schedule and reducing on-site trades during the busiest quarter of the academic year.
