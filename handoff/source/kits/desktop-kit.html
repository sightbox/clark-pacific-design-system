Accent bar
3px × 40px, flush under the photo. Blue and Orange alternate across a row. Optional — omit on dense grids.

Meta row
12px SemiBold uppercase Mid Gray, type and date separated by a middot. Optional.
04
Title
20px SemiBold uppercase Dark Charcoal, three lines maximum. Always present.
05
Body
15px / 24px Regular Dark Charcoal, two to three lines. Optional.
06
Read More
Track spans the full column width; a 40px Blue or Orange bar sits under the label and fills the track left to right on hover. 34px above.

The card raises nothing and shifts nothing. Only the accent bar animates — the image darkens only in the image-as-button variant.

CARBONSHIELD® / Desktop
CARBONSHIELD PHOTO · 4:3
Proprietary Concrete Mix
Building for the future

CARBONSHIELD® is a unique and proprietary concrete mix developed by Clark Pacific that provides the same performance as traditional mixes and is standard in our structural products.

It replaces 25% of the portland cement in our concrete mixes, reducing embodied carbon by roughly 20%. Our goal is double this by 2030, through our continuous R&D efforts and material partnerships.
