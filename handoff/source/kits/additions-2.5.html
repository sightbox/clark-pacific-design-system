Project Overview
–

UC Davis Health needed 1,200 new stalls adjacent to the medical center without disrupting an active campus. Clark Pacific delivered the structure design-build — precast frame, architectural facade panels, and a photovoltaic-ready roof deck — fourteen weeks after mobilization.

Off-site fabrication ran parallel to foundations, cutting roughly five months from a conventional schedule and reducing on-site trades during the busiest quarter of the academic year.
