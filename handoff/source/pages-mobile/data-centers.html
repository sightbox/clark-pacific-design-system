Why precast works for mission-critical data centers

Our offsite manufacturing approach reduces overall project risk through improved cost and schedule certainty and greater supply chain control — without compromising a project's overall design.

Factory-fabricated structural components arrive on-site ready to erect, running parallel to foundation and MEP work to compress the critical path by two to four months on a typical 100MW facility.
