True Owner Value
Durable. Versatile. Economical.

Architectural precast concrete has quietly revolutionized the built environment — from city centers to industrial parks — creating a dramatic range of architectural expression across the West Coast and the nation.

Our Architectural Precast provides aesthetic versatility and an economical choice — cost-effective production without sacrificing design.

Supports embedded materials (brick, terracotta, stone)
Ideal for blast and ballistics performance requirements
CEC Energy Efficiency Category: Light Mass
Durable

Superior resistance to fire, weather, and impact. A long service life with minimal maintenance compared to other cladding types.

Aesthetic Flexibility

Virtually limitless design potential — reveals, patterns, custom colors, textures, and embedded materials at minimal additional cost.

Low Maintenance

Precast concrete requires minimal maintenance over its lifespan — reducing operating costs for owners over the life of the building.

Economical

Cost-effective production at scale — factory efficiency and single-source delivery reduce overall project cost without sacrificing design quality.

Design Potential
Virtually limitless aesthetic versatility

Custom-made forms create concrete panels in precise dimensions and shapes. Reveals, joints, patterns, and other expressive detailing are introduced at the panel surface — all in the factory, before delivery to site.

Specific color effects are achieved through varying sands, aggregates, and pigments. Stone, tile, or brick veneers can be attached, cast into, or replicated in architectural precast panels at the plant — allowing dramatic aesthetic effects at minimal additional cost.
