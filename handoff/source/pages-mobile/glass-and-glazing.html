The Factory Advantage
Glass installed in the factory, not the field

Traditional glazing installation is one of the most weather-dependent, labor-intensive, and schedule-sensitive phases of envelope construction. Clark Pacific eliminates that risk by installing glass in a controlled factory environment — then delivering fully-glazed panels to site ready to crane into position.

Factory-installed glazing delivers tighter tolerances, consistent quality across every panel, and dramatically faster on-site installation — compressing the overall envelope schedule.

One-off: Glazing Configurations
Glazing configurations
Punched Openings
IF 75 Glazing

Standard punched window openings within the opaque panel system. The most cost-effective glazing configuration — ideal for projects balancing views with thermal performance.

Clear, low-e, or solar control glass
Factory-sealed at the opening
Supports any sill/jamb/head detail
Enhanced Vision
IF 85 Glazing

Window openings aligned to the top of the panel — minimizing horizontal sill joints and increasing interior vision height by approximately 6". More light, less bulk.

~6" more vision glass vs. IF 75
Reduced visible horizontal transitions
Same frame and connection system
Full Height Glass
IF 100 Glazing

Continuous floor-to-ceiling exterior glass — no sealant joint at the floor line. The premium glazing configuration for the most demanding architectural expressions.

Full-height continuous exterior glass
No floor-line sealant joint
Maximizes interior daylighting
