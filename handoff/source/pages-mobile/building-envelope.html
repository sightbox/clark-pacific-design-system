Our Mission
Reduce the complexity of envelope delivery

The building envelope is the most difficult part of any building to design. Design teams are expected to meet client aesthetic goals while satisfying energy codes, waterproofing requirements, and structural constraints — often with multiple subcontractors and conflicting schedules.

Our mission is to turn the building envelope into one of the most rewarding parts of your project — by delivering it as a single-source prefabricated system, designed and built to meet every performance requirement before it leaves the factory.

Advantages of prefabricated building envelopes
Lower Risk

Single-source accountability eliminates the finger-pointing between trades that drives most envelope failures.

Reduce Costs

Factory efficiency and parallel fabrication reduce total installed cost vs. traditional multi-subcontractor delivery.

Increase Efficiency

Panels fabricated offsite while foundation and structure proceed — compressing the overall project schedule.

Reduce Site Impacts

Fewer trades on-site, less scaffolding, and just-in-time delivery reduce congestion and site safety exposures.

Less Construction Waste

Factory production generates a fraction of the waste produced by field-built envelope systems.

Uncompromised Design

Full-height windows, complex reveals, custom finishes — prefabricated systems deliver architectural ambition at scale.

Budget Certainty

Fixed-price prefabricated scope locks in cost early and protects the owner from field change orders.

Higher Quality Control

Controlled plant environment delivers consistent tolerances, finish quality, and weatherproofing panel to panel.
