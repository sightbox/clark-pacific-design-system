About This Webinar

Discover how prefabricated parking structures and the Precast Hybrid Moment Frame (PHMF) are transforming construction with enhanced performance and seismic resiliency.

In this on-demand session, our team walks through the structural advantages of the PHMF system — how it allows larger spans, reduces lateral load transfer, and delivers inherent ductility for high-seismic zones like California.

Whether you're a structural engineer, developer, or architect evaluating precast for your next parking project, this session gives you the data to make the case for resilient design.

What You'll Learn
How PHMF reduces seismic load transfer and expands design flexibility
Schedule and cost advantages of prefabricated vs. cast-in-place parking
Case studies: real projects where resilient design added value
Q&A with Clark Pacific's structural and design-assist engineers
