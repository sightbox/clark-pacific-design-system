Our Commitments
How we demonstrate sustainability every day
Low-Carbon Mix Designs

Supplementary cementitious materials replace up to 40% of Portland cement, dramatically reducing embodied carbon in every panel we produce.

Zero-Waste Manufacturing

100% diversion of metal, wood, and concrete scrap from landfill across all four production facilities through reuse and recycling partnerships.

Factory Efficiency

Controlled off-site manufacturing eliminates redundant jobsite deliveries, material overruns, and weather-related waste inherent to field construction.

Environmental Product Declarations

Third-party verified EPDs published for Architectural Precast, Infinite Facade®, and GFRC — so your team has the data you need for LEED and reporting.

LEED Contribution

Clark Pacific products support LEED v4 credits across Materials & Resources, Energy & Atmosphere, and Indoor Environmental Quality categories.

NetZero Building Platform

Our integrated structural and envelope system is designed to help project teams model and achieve net-zero operational carbon performance targets.

Responsible Manufacturing
Sustainability starts in the factory

Off-site manufacturing inherently reduces environmental impact. Our controlled factory environment eliminates jobsite waste, minimizes redundant material deliveries, and dramatically reduces the embodied carbon of construction.

Every facility operates a zero-landfill scrap program — metal, wood, and concrete byproducts are 100% diverted through reuse and recycling partnerships.
