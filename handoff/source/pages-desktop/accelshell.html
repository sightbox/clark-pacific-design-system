What is AccelShell™?
–

AccelShell™ is Clark Pacific's prefabricated insulated wall panel and enclosure solution for data centers and other critical facilities. The system combines a durable precast concrete exterior, continuous insulation, interior concrete wythe, engineered connections, and coordinated joint detailing into a factory-produced wall assembly.
