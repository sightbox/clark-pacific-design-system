From concrete to complete building systems

Founded as Tecon Pacific in 1963, Clark Pacific began in West Sacramento on a four-acre site with fifteen employees and James Clark as its general manager. With the support of dedicated engineering, manufacturing facilities, and field teams, the company grew rapidly during the early years of the industry in California. As new methods emerged for forming, casting, finishing, shipping, and on-site installation, Clark Pacific built those capabilities into a disciplined, repeatable delivery model used across diverse project types on the West Coast.

Today, Clark Pacific is owned and managed by Jim Clark’s sons, Donald Clark and Robert Clark. They are committed to advancing prefabricated building systems with an emphasis on performance, resilience, and better project outcomes.

A third generation of the Clark family is now helping lead the company’s next chapter, expanding nationally to support large-scale programs with an emphasis on speed, sustainability, and disciplined execution.
