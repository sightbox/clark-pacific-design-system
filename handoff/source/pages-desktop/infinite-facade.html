Infinite Facade® offers a highly customizable, prefabricated building envelope solution that streamlines design and construction. With customizable aesthetics, accommodating full-height windows, and a single-source approach, it reduces risk while delivering a high-performance facade with architectural freedom.

System Overview + Materials
System overview

Infinite Facade® combines precast, glazing and weather barrier into one panelized envelope, designed, manufactured and installed by a single team.

Panels arrive on site finished and sealed, so the building closes in weeks earlier, with fewer trades on the wall and one warranty for the whole envelope.

System Configurations
Designed as one. Built to perform.
IF 75
Most Cost-Effective
IF75 DIAGRAM

Punched window openings surrounded by your choice of opaque finishes. The most economical configuration for projects prioritizing budget certainty.

IF 85
More Vision Glass
IF85 DIAGRAM

Punched openings align with the top of the panel — minimizing horizontal joints and providing approximately 6" of additional vision glass from the interior.

IF 100
Full Height Glass
IF100 DIAGRAM

Eliminates sealant joints at the floor line — providing full height, continuous exterior glass. The premium configuration for the most demanding architectural expressions.

Design Flexibility
Exterior finish options are limitless

In addition to the wide variety of colors and textures available in traditional precast, other materials can be cast into the exterior veneer — brick, terracotta, and stone. Form liners allow an endless number of geometric patterns and reveals.

Infinite Facade® is a standardized panel system that allows design teams to focus on aesthetics. It redefines how owners and design teams approach the facade system — without the coordination risk of traditional multi-trade delivery.
