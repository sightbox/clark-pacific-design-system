For Immediate Release

Clark Pacific, California's leading manufacturer of prefabricated building systems, today announced the expansion of its operations into the Pacific Northwest and Southwest, opening new offices in Washington and Arizona to meet rising demand for off-site construction solutions.

The strategic expansion reflects the company's long-term commitment to bringing factory-built structural, envelope, and parking systems to markets where demand for faster, more predictable construction delivery is accelerating — driven by data center construction, healthcare investment, and life sciences growth.

"The Pacific Northwest and Arizona are two of the fastest-growing construction markets in the country, and both are hungry for the kind of schedule certainty and single-source accountability that prefabricated systems deliver."

Ron Ball — CEO, Clark Pacific

The new Washington office, based in the Seattle metropolitan area, will serve data center developers and healthcare clients across Washington, Oregon, and Idaho. The Arizona office, located in the Phoenix metro area, will support the growing semiconductor, data center, and multifamily markets across the Southwest.

Both offices will be staffed with business development, design-assist engineering, and project management teams, leveraging Clark Pacific's existing manufacturing capacity in California while developing regional relationships for the company's planned Pacific Northwest manufacturing facility, announced earlier this year.
